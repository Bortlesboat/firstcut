Privacy policy

Draft updated November 8, 2026

This policy describes the First Cut website and First Cut Publisher, our internal video publishing tool.

First Cut operates this prelaunch website and publishing tool. Contact setup is in progress. The tool is not open to public registration, and no connected-account publishing service has launched.

Website visitors

This static website does not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or embedded social feeds. It does not have accounts or a sign-up form. GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The planned publishing workflow also stages approved MP4 videos on GitHub Pages so a publishing provider can retrieve them. Staging makes the video publicly accessible before a social post exists. Anyone with its URL can download it. Files remain available until removed from the published site; copies may remain in repository history or third-party caches.

Connected accounts

First Cut Publisher is used by the First Cut account owner. Account connections authorize uploads or posts chosen by that owner. The planned provider for Instagram, TikTok and X is Buffer; account activation and live validation are pending. First Cut Publisher does not ask for social account passwords.

  • Instagram: owner-authorized publishing through a connected Buffer channel.
  • TikTok: owner-authorized automatic publishing through a connected Buffer channel. A separate direct inbox-upload option requires the owner to finish the post in TikTok; access to that direct option is not established.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the authorized channel using the YouTube API Services.
  • X: owner-authorized publishing through a connected Buffer channel.

The tool does not collect direct messages, contacts, follower lists, comments or advertising data.

Data stored and its purpose

On the operator’s computer, the tool stores access and refresh tokens, app credentials needed for token renewal, available account identifiers and connection or expiry timestamps. These allow authorized API calls and token renewal.

When Buffer is activated, local credentials include its API key and the selected channel identifiers. Buffer holds the connected platforms’ authorization and handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Local story files include media, scripts, captions, sources and asset credits. Posting records include the platform, time, returned post URL or upload identifier, the owner’s approval text and limited error information. These records help track delivery and diagnose failures. They are separate from the credential store.

A contact address will be listed before publishing begins. Once available, correspondence will be processed to respond to requests or investigate corrections.

Sharing and use

When Buffer is activated, the tool sends it public media URLs, captions, channel identifiers and publishing metadata, including relevant AI disclosures. Buffer retrieves the media and submits approved posts to the connected platforms. Direct integrations send selected media and relevant title, caption, description or disclosure metadata to the chosen platform. TikTok inbox uploads require the owner to finish the post in TikTok. Public posts are visible according to the destination platform’s settings.

First Cut does not sell connected-account data, use it for targeted advertising or use it to train AI models. Platform data is used only for the publishing functions described here. Local access is limited to the operator and authorized people maintaining the tool.

Each platform handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. See TikTok’s privacy policy, Instagram’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy and X’s privacy policy.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Credentials remain in local storage until the operator removes the connection. Revoking access in a platform’s settings prevents further authorized use but does not automatically erase local files. The operator must also remove the corresponding local credentials. Tokens can expire or be revoked by the platform.

To disconnect the Buffer route, the operator must remove the connected channels in Buffer, revoke the Buffer API key and remove its local credentials. Removing local credentials alone does not revoke Buffer’s platform access or cancel posts held by Buffer. Those must be managed in Buffer and the destination platform. Staged videos do not expire automatically and require separate removal from the public host.

Story files and posting receipts remain until the operator deletes them; the prototype does not have an automatic retention schedule. Before publishing begins, this page will list a contact address for access, correction or deletion requests. Do not send passwords or tokens. We may ask for information needed to verify a request.

Google authorization can be revoked through Google Account permissions. Access to other platforms can be revoked through their connected-app settings. Removing local records does not delete an already published video; use the destination platform’s controls for that.

Security

Credentials are stored outside website files and are not intended to be published or included in story output. They are kept in a local file, not an encrypted application vault. The operator is responsible for protecting the computer and backups. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

Children and changes

The publishing tool is not offered to children and does not provide public registration. We may revise this policy as the tool changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
